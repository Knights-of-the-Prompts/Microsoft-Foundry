    Value attribution tells us what an agent contributed. Cost attribution tells us what it consumed. Agent reporting tells us who needs to know. The control plane determines what should happen next.

    The Governance Chain

    A KPI defines the required insight.
    ↓
    The required insight defines the required signals.
    ↓
    The required signals define the connector tools.
    ↓
    The connector tools define the required access.
    ↓
    The control plane checks whether the persona is ready to act.
