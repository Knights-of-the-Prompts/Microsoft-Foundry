## Password & Account Access

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Go to https://aka.ms/sspr (Self-Service Password Reset). Enter your work email and follow the on-screen steps. If SSPR is unavailable for your account, contact helpdesk@contoso.com.

**Q: My account is locked. What do I do?**
Accounts lock after 5 failed login attempts. Wait 15 minutes for automatic unlock, or contact helpdesk@contoso.com for immediate assistance.

**Q: How do I set up multi-factor authentication (MFA)?**
Visit https://aka.ms/mfasetup with your work account. Register the Microsoft Authenticator app, a phone number, or a hardware key. MFA is required for all remote access.

## VPN & Remote Access

**Q: How do I connect to the VPN?**
Install GlobalProtect from the Contoso software portal. Enter gateway address `vpn.contoso.com` and authenticate with your work credentials and MFA.

**Q: The VPN is slow or keeps disconnecting. What should I try?**
1. Restart the GlobalProtect client.
2. Verify your local internet connection.
3. Disconnect and reconnect.
4. If the issue persists, contact helpdesk@contoso.com with your location and device model.

## Hardware

**Q: My laptop is not starting. What should I do?**
Hold the power button for 10 seconds to force shutdown, then press it once to restart. If the laptop still does not start, contact helpdesk@contoso.com or visit the nearest IT desk.

**Q: How do I request a new device or accessory?**
Submit a request at https://servicedesk.contoso.com > Hardware Request. Manager approval is required for devices over $200.

---

## Microsoft 365 & Teams

**Q: I cannot access my email. What should I try?**
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Clear your browser cache or try a different browser.
3. Visit https://outlook.office.com directly.
4. If the issue persists, contact helpdesk@contoso.com.

**Q: How do I add an external participant to a Teams meeting?**
In the meeting invite, type the external email address in the attendees field. Ensure your meeting is set to allow external guests (check Teams meeting options before sending).

**Q: How do I install approved software?**
Open the Company Portal app on your device. Browse the catalog and click Install. Apps listed there are pre-approved and require no additional IT approval.
